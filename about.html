About me

I design brands and I sell. Most of my best work happens where those two meet.

My story

My career started with a year as a UX Design Intern at Walmart Global Tech, working alongside product, engineering, and research teams on cart, checkout, and product pages. I then joined Movista in Bentonville, Arkansas as a Marketing Design Intern, where I designed ads, event materials, and an AI in Retail white paper, and led the redesign of the company mascot.

From there I studied and worked at BYU-Idaho, designing print and digital projects and mentoring 20+ students daily in Adobe Creative Cloud. I now study Marketing at Brigham Young University in Provo, Utah, with a minor in Graphic Design, and I expect to graduate in April 2028.

Along the way I built full professional proficiency in Spanish. That fits the work I did leading 25 content creators across two social pages, one of them Spanish-language, and coaching about 200 volunteers in social outreach.

Design taught me how a brand should look and feel. Sales taught me whether it actually works on a real customer. In summer 2026 I closed $530K+ as an Inside Sales Closer at Aptive Environmental, and I bring that same results focus back to brand strategy at Boundless Fit and in my freelance work.
